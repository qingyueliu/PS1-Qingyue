# Separate instructor guidance used for v2

Source: Prof. Luyao Zhang's Week 1 and Week 2 comments, as supplied by Qingyue Liu during this revision session. This file summarizes adopted guidance; it is not a third peer review.

- Week 1: start with a truthful-observer baseline, then change one reporting incentive; ask who benefits from advice.
- Week 2: check unexpected patterns across seeds and learning settings; isolate the observer's incentive while keeping other settings fixed.
- Application here: preserve the existing truthful/forced-use benchmark, add the cooperation bonus, and run all five exploration levels across 30 seeds.
- Limit: the current baseline is not a reproduction of Leung et al.'s population/partner-selection model. Reproducing that paper remains future work.
